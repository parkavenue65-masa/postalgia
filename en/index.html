Postalgia
A daily letter from your past photos

At set times each day, a letter arrives from your old photos. Answer one question about the photo, and you get to see it.

Common questions

No letters are arriving

In your phone's Settings app, check that notifications are allowed for Postalgia. You can change the delivery times in the app's Settings.

No photos appear, or the same kind of photos keep coming

In your phone's Settings app, let Postalgia see your photos. On iPhone, choose "Allow Full Access" so letters can come from all of your photos.

There is a photo I don't want to see

On the photo screen, tap "Don't send". That photo will not arrive again. The photo on your phone is not deleted. You can let it arrive again from the app's Settings.

I want to start over

At the bottom of the app's Settings, use "Clear records and start over". Cleared records cannot be brought back. Your photos and settings stay as they are.

Does it cost money?

You can try everything free for the first 7 days. After that, pay $4.99 once and keep using it for good. There is no monthly fee. Starting the trial never charges you automatically.

Do I need to buy it again on a new phone?

No. Install the app with the same Apple Account and tap "Restore purchase".

Are my photos sent anywhere?

No. Your photos are used only on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
