Privacy Policy

This page explains how Postalgia ("the app") handles your photos and records.

1. We do not collect personal information

The app does not send or collect your name, email address, photos, records, or anything else. There is no server run by us, and the app contains no analytics or advertising.

2. Your photos

To deliver letters, the app reads the photos on your phone. Checking a photo, such as how many faces it has or what it shows (food, scenery, and so on), is all done on your phone. Your photos are never sent off your phone.

3. Where a photo was taken

When a photo has location information, the app passes that location to your phone's place-name lookup (provided by Apple on iPhone and by Google on Android) to show a place name. That information is handled under Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

4. Your records

Your answers, stars, and settings are saved only on your phone. Deleting the app also deletes them.

5. Sharing a photo

Only when you tap "Share" does the app use your phone's sharing feature to send the photo to the person or app you choose. Before sending, location and other shooting details are removed from the photo. A shared photo is then handled under the rules of the app you sent it to.

6. Notifications

Letter notifications are scheduled on your phone. No outside server is used.

7. Dropbox (Android only)

On Android, the app reads photos in Dropbox only if you connect Dropbox yourself. Photos are loaded straight from Dropbox to your phone and are never sent to us. The key used for the connection is kept in secure storage on your phone. You can disconnect anytime in Settings.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.

9. Contact

Please use the contact address on the support page.
